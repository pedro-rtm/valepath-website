# Valepath — Brand Guide

**Domain:** valepath.com
**Practice active:** 2013—present
**Design register:** Warm stone and aged copper — inherited from the former Velkan identity
**Lineage:** derived in 2026 from the Personal Design Language v3.0. Lineage only: the governing input for this brand is its audience (PE investment committees and operating partners); design policy is audience-first per `Stuff/PORTFOLIO-GOVERNANCE.md`.

---

## I. Name

**Valepath** — from *value* + *path*. The path to technology-driven value creation. Direct, compositional, self-explanatory. The name maps to every phase of the business: assessing value (DD), delivering value (post-deal execution), predicting value (intelligence platform).

**Pronunciation:** VAL-path (two syllables, natural English).

**Legal form:** Valepath Ltd (UK) or Valepath as a trading name. Never "Valepath Consulting," "Valepath Advisory," or any suffix. The name stands alone.

---

## II. Positioning Context

Valepath is a technology-transformation practice. Its work spans enterprise systems, ERP, CRM, infrastructure, data, automation, AI, operating models, and technology due diligence. The brand must support the full practice rather than reducing it to AI or transaction advisory. It must work across these expressions:

| Phase | Brand expression |
|---|---|
| Enterprise transformation | "Valepath rebuilt the operating system behind the business" |
| Technology due diligence | "We retained Valepath for technology due diligence" |
| Post-deal execution | "Valepath delivered the 90-day integration sprint" |
| Productized modules | "Valepath Cloud Cost Reset" |
| Benchmark product | "Valepath Intelligence" / "Valepath Portfolio Benchmark" |
| Predictive platform | "Valepath predicts technology-driven value creation outcomes" |

The visual identity must feel institutional and permanent from day one — not a startup finding itself, but a practice that has always existed.

## IV. Typography

### Wordmark

**Instrument Serif** — Regular (400). It carries the established transformation identity into the Valepath name and supplies warmth without ornament.

- Letter-spacing: `-0.02em`
- Weight: 400 for the wordmark and display headings
- The wordmark is paired with a narrow aged-copper vertical bar.

### Body & UI

**Space Grotesk** — Regular (400) for body copy and Medium/SemiBold (500/600) for labels, navigation, controls, and compact data. It gives the serif display voice a precise operational counterweight.

### Hierarchy

| Level | Font | Weight | Size | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | Instrument Serif | 400 | 44px reference | -0.02em |
| H1 | Instrument Serif | 400 | φ^4 base | -0.025em |
| H2 | Instrument Serif | 400 | φ^3 base | -0.02em |
| H3 | Instrument Serif | 400 | φ^2 base | -0.01em |
| Body | Space Grotesk | 400 | base (16–18px) | normal |
| Caption | Space Grotesk | 500 | φ^-1 base | 0.02em |
| Compact mark | Instrument Serif | 400 | 44px reference | normal |

### Rules

- Type scale follows golden ratio (φ = 1.618) per design.md.
- Line height: 1.5 for body, 1.2 for headings.
- Paragraph width: 60–75 characters.
- No all-caps except in the compact mark and metadata labels.

## V. Visual Identity Elements

### Wordmark

The wordmark is “Valepath” set in Instrument Serif Regular (400), preceded by a three-pixel Aged Copper vertical rule. The rule is the primary identity device: a measured intervention point and a direct inheritance from the transformation practice’s former Velkan identity.

| Variant | Background | Text | Bar |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Warm Stone / Paper White | Ink Navy `#1a1e2e` | Aged Copper `#a0714f` |
| Dark application | Ink Navy | Warm Stone `#faf8f5` | Aged Copper `#a0714f` |

### Compact Mark

The letter “V” in Instrument Serif, paired with the aged-copper vertical bar. The favicon may use an italic serif “V” in copper on Warm Stone when the bar would be illegible.

### Clear Space

Minimum clear space around the wordmark equals the cap height of the "V". No elements may intrude into this zone.

### Minimum Size

- Wordmark: never below 120px wide (digital) or 30mm (print).
- Compact mark: never below 24px (digital) or 8mm (print).

## VI. Tone of Voice

### Character

Valepath speaks like a senior engineer presenting to an Investment Committee: precise, quantified, opinionated, and respectful of the audience's time.

### Principles

| Principle | Means | Does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| **Quantified** | Every claim has a number attached. "GBP 2.4M of technical debt" not "significant technical debt" | Drowning in data. Numbers serve the argument, not replace it |
| **Opinionated** | "We recommend against this acquisition" not "there are considerations on both sides" | Reckless. Opinions are grounded in methodology |
| **Economical** | Say it once, say it clearly, move on | Terse to the point of obscurity |
| **Institutional** | "Valepath assessed..." not "I looked at..." First person plural, not singular | Pompous. The firm voice is a vehicle for clarity, not self-importance |

### Language rules

- **First person plural.** "We assessed," "our methodology," "Valepath recommends." Never "I" in client-facing materials.
- **Active voice.** "The architecture presents three scalability risks" not "three scalability risks were identified."
- **GBP for all financial figures.** Target market is UK PE. EUR only when explicitly discussing European operations.
- **No jargon without definition.** If a term would require a PE operating partner to Google it, define it on first use.
- **No superlatives.** Never "best-in-class," "world-class," "cutting-edge," "state-of-the-art." Describe what it does, not what it claims to be.

## VII. Applications

### DD Report

The flagship deliverable uses Warm Stone and Paper White surfaces, Instrument Serif display headings, Space Grotesk body copy, and restrained copper rules. RAG scores use the functional palette. The executive summary remains on page 1 and is designed to lift directly into an IC memo.

### Website (valepath.com)

Warm-stone theme with paper-white surfaces, ink text, copper accents, Instrument Serif headings, and Space Grotesk body copy. The site presents the complete transformation record—enterprise systems, infrastructure, data, automation, AI, and due diligence—not one narrow offer.

Professional headshot + Valepath wordmark on cover. Content in personal voice (Pedro), DD methodology content attributed to Valepath.

### One-Pager

## IX. What Valepath Is Not

- Not a startup. No gradient logos, playful colors, or inflated claims.
- Not a Big 4 brand. No red, no blue-and-white corporate, no stock photography of handshakes.
- Not a tech company. No circuit board motifs, no "digital" aesthetic, no neon accents.
- Not an AI wrapper. AI is one capability within a much broader transformation practice.

Valepath is an operating practice for consequential technology change. The brand should feel established, exact, warm without sentimentality, and immediately credible.
